## Observations

SVE (Scalable Vector Extensions) is ARM's successor to NEON

Two different CPU cores: 
- 10 × Cortex-X925: high power
- 10 × Cortex-A725: low power

performance analysis is simple because there's one thread per core

Cluster A: X925 cores, share: 12 MB L3

Cluster B: A725 cores, share: 12 MB L3

GPU: NVIDIA GB10
